RIVAL ("we", "us") is a social fitness app that lets you log workouts, earn
Effort, and train alongside your teams. This policy explains what information
RIVAL collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. We collect the minimum
we need to run the app, we don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

## What we collect

**Account information.** Email address, display name, optional username, and
optional profile photo when you create an account.

**Fitness activity data.** Workouts you log manually, scan from photos, or sync
from Strava: activity type, date/time, duration, distance, elevation, workout
details (e.g. exercises, sets, weights), effort scores, personal records, race
entries and finish times, goals, and streaks. If an activity has a GPS route
(e.g. from Strava), we store its route outline to draw maps on share cards.

**Photos and videos.** Images you attach to workouts, upload for workout
scanning, or use to create AI share cards. These are stored so they can appear
in your activity history and team feed.

**Social content.** Messages, comments, reactions ("Respect"/"Inspired"),
encouragements, and planned sessions you post inside your teams.

**Device tokens.** A push-notification token, if you enable notifications.

We do **not** collect your precise live location, contacts, or advertising
identifiers.

## Strava connection

If you connect Strava, we use Strava's official API to import your activities
(including new ones as you record them) and your athlete name. You can
disconnect Strava at any time from your profile, and choose whether to also
delete the activities that were imported. Our use of Strava data follows the
Strava API Agreement.

## AI features

Two features send data to AI providers to work:

- **Workout scanning** sends the photo you choose (e.g. a whiteboard or gym
  screen) to an AI service to read the workout details out of it.
- **AI share cards** send your chosen photo, workout stats, and optional
  caption to AI services (OpenAI for image generation, fal.ai for image
  enhancement) to create the shareable image.

These providers process the content to produce the result; we don't use your
content to train AI models. **If a photo includes other people, please make
sure they're okay with it being uploaded and (for share cards) turned into an
image you may share publicly.**

## Who can see your information

- **Your teammates** see your name, avatar, activities, effort scores,
  reactions, comments, and chat within teams you've joined.
- **Public teams** are discoverable by name, logo, and member count; joining
  them still requires admin approval.
- **Share cards you export** go wherever you post them — that part is up to you.
- **Service providers** that run RIVAL's infrastructure process data on our
  behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage), Vercel (web
  hosting), Strava (if connected), OpenAI and fal.ai (AI features), Expo
  (push notifications), and Resend (account emails).

We never sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone
else except as required by law.

## Deleting your data

- You can delete individual activities, photos, goals, and races in the app.
- Disconnecting Strava offers to remove everything it imported.
- You can permanently delete your account and all associated data any time
  from **Profile → Delete account**. Teams you created are handed to another
  member (or deleted if you were the only one). If you have any trouble,
  contact us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]** and we'll complete it within 14 days.

## Data retention and security

Your data is kept while your account is active and deleted when your account
is deleted. Data is stored with Supabase and protected in transit with TLS and
at rest with row-level access controls, so users can only reach data they're
entitled to see.

## Children

RIVAL is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in
your country), and we don't knowingly collect their information.
